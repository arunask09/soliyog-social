## LinkedIn — launch post

> Founder first-person. If you have a real 2-line anecdote (a friend/cousin you helped
> who kept getting rejected), swap it in as the opening — a true story outperforms the
> observation. Pair with the `insight-card-1200x628` template or a screen recording of
> the guides.

Most freshers prep for a version of the job that doesn't exist.

They grind DSA problems for a data analyst role that's mostly SQL and stakeholder questions. They rehearse "tell me about yourself" for an equity research interview that's really testing whether they can defend a number.

The gap isn't effort. It's information. Nobody tells you what a role involves day to day, or what each interview round is actually screening for.

That's why we built Soliyog.

It pairs two things freshers usually hunt for separately:

• 210+ live openings in IT and finance, filtered for roles open to people with 0–2 years of experience
• 8 guides that break down what a role actually does, and what recruiters look for at each stage

Plus 2 structured tracks if you'd rather follow a path than a pile of links.

No salary promises. No "crack any interview in 30 days." Just an honest picture of the job, and a way to prepare for the one that exists.

If you're in your final year or your first job hunt — or you know someone who is — take a look: soliyog.com

Genuinely want your feedback on the guides.
