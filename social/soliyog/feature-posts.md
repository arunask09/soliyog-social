> Card: `templates/feature-cv-builder-1080x1350.html` → `templates/exports/feature-cv-builder-1080x1350.jpg`

### LinkedIn

Most fresher resumes get filtered before a person ever reads them.

Not because the candidate wasn't good enough. Because the file didn't parse. Two-column layouts, tables, graphics: applicant tracking systems choke on all of it, and the resume never reaches a human.

We just shipped a free resume builder inside Soliyog to fix that.

• Single-column, real text: the format ATS systems actually parse
• Live preview as you type, built to fit one page
• Sections for education, experience, projects, certificates, skills: the structure recruiters expect from a fresher resume
• Bold your keywords inline, export straight to PDF
• Everything stays in your browser. Nothing gets uploaded anywhere

No sign-up needed to try it. Fill in your own details, or load the sample resume first to see the format.

If you're prepping applications this season, this is one less thing to get wrong: soliyog.com/tools/cv-builder
